Why Bharosa Enforces Strict Workspace & Role Separation

Dual-sided role-based access control (RBAC) ensures legal compliance under the Indian Contract Act, 1872

Cryptographic Data Isolation

Freelancer earnings, private estimates, and notes are strictly excluded from client API responses. Milestone deliverables are verified via irreversible SHA-256 checksums.

72-Hour Anti-Ghosting Protocol

If a client becomes unresponsive after deliverable submission, timelines freeze automatically. After 72 hours, platform arbiters can release escrow to protect contractor cashflow.

Guaranteed Escrow Protection

Neither party can modify funds in transit without mutual cryptographic consent or neutral mediator sign-off. Indian GST compliance is baked into every released milestone.

🚀 How to Connect Your Supabase Project:

  1. Go to supabase.com and create or select a project.
  2. Open the SQL Editor in Supabase, paste the contents of supabase-schema.sql, and click Run.
  3. From Project Settings → API, copy your Project URL and anon public API Key.
  4. Paste them into your local .env file: 
    SUPABASE_URL=https://your-project.supabase.co
SUPABASE_KEY=your-anon-or-service-role-key
